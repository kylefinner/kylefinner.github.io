About this catalog

How catalogs are combined
  • Nothing is discarded. Every entry of every input catalog is kept with its published values; the table under each cluster lists them all.
  • Matching. Entries are grouped when their separation is below 3σ of the combined position errors, or ≤0.5 Mpc (≤0.75 Mpc if an optical/IR centre is involved), within 1′–10′.
  • SZ & X-ray detections at the same position trace the same gas and are always grouped; if their redshifts disagree the cluster is flagged z conflict.
  • Optical/IR systems must also agree in redshift (|Δz| ≤ 0.1(1+z), 0.03 if both spectroscopic), so projections stay separate. At most one entry per catalog per cluster.
  • Adopted values: z prefers spectroscopic, then curated SZ follow-up; M500 prefers SPT, then ACT, X-ray, Planck. Each adopted value names its source.
Mass caveats
  • M500c values are shown exactly as published; they are not rescaled to a common calibration.
  • Typical offsets in this catalog: ACT DR6 ≈ 5% below SPT (≈10% at z > 1); Planck PSZ2 and MCXC (hydrostatic scale) ≈ 20% below SPT/ACT.
  • Weak-lensing masses come from LC2 (Sereno 2015) and the paper tables of MC2 (Finner, Jee et al.), SPT/HST (Schrabback+18/21, Zohren+22, Kim+19), Jee+11, CLASH, LoCuSS, CCCP+MENeaCS and HSC-XXL. They are their own mass family and are never adopted over SZ/X-ray masses.
  • Headline WL mass: the MC2 analysis if available, otherwise the paper's preferred value from the most recent paper; every other measurement is listed with its bibcode. M500c converted from M200c (NFW) where a paper gives only M200c. The SPT/HST masses are maximum-likelihood values without the papers' mass-bias correction.
  • R500 and θ500 are derived from the adopted M500 and z with Planck18 and are indicative only.
Citing

Please cite the original catalogs for any values you use; each catalog name links to its paper. The build pipeline is public: cluster-dashboard.
